Verified Problem Statement

"Many rural entrepreneurs face difficulties in accessing and understanding digital financial services such as fintech platforms, online credit, insurance, digital payments, and investment opportunities. Limited awareness and lack of accessible financial guidance make it difficult for them to identify suitable financial products and make informed financial decisions. As a result, rural businesses may struggle to obtain adequate capital, protect themselves against financial risks, and invest in opportunities required for business expansion. Despite the availability of various digital financial services, there is a lack of simple, accessible, and entrepreneur-focused financial support that connects rural entrepreneurs with relevant digital financial tools and opportunities. Therefore, there is a need for a system that can improve awareness and accessibility of digital financial services, helping rural entrepreneurs make informed financial decisions and access suitable opportunities for business growth."
